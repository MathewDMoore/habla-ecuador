About Habla Ecuador

Habla Ecuador 🇪🇨

Language, culture & connection.

Our mission

Learn to speak with people, not just translate words. Ecuadorian Spanish comes first, with room for Bolivia, Venezuela, Cuba, Mexico, other regional varieties, and U.S. / U.K. English.

How a song becomes language evidence

  1. Confirm the artist, Ecuador connection, genre, region, and date.
  2. Show full lyrics only when their license permits reuse; otherwise store a short observation and write an original example.
  3. Cross-check the meaning in CORPHA, the Ecuadorian Academy, ASALE, or another documented source.
  4. Label the register, intensity, generation, region, and whether it sounds natural in conversation.
  5. Write a new Habla Ecuador example and send uncertain usage for Ecuadorian review.

Usage & sources

Usage type describes how an expression works; tone describes its strength. An idiom can also be informal or vulgar. Country labels cover this entry, not every sense or every city. Read Meaning by country / region before choosing a sense. Choose U.S. or U.K. English to browse English meanings with their Spanish counterparts. Choose the Spanish counterpart region separately. Clinical and literal meanings have separate categories; their availability under a country does not make them local slang. These views index the current Spanish collection; shared Spanish pairs have country review pending. Strong English phrases keep vulgar Spanish counterparts. Tone labels describe the Spanish source; English wording may differ in strength. Meanings depend on the sentence, relationship and tone. English options are editorial glosses, not automatic replacements. Linked dictionaries and studies support the noted usage; they do not certify every city or accent. Caracas and Lima currently have country guidance; city review is pending. Entries stay available offline after the app has loaded; source links require internet. Voices depend on your device.
