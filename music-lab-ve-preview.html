Translation accuracy & regional notes / Notas

“Le pedí a Dios” means “I asked God”, not “I said goodbye”. “Que me pusiera en tu camino” means “to put me in your path”; the person moving is the speaker, not the beloved. “Menos por los amores…” is grammatically unclear, so its interpretation is provisional. The word “culo” is deliberately direct and vulgar; it is not euphemised in the English rendering.
