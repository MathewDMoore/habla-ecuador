# Original English speaking lessons — build 130

Mathew found the build 129 Spanish-to-English clip promotional. Both external English video catalogs are removed from the learning section. English learning now uses original, self-paced teaching inside Habla: a Spanish situation and explanation, an English example/reply with Spanish meaning, model playback by whole reply and short chunks, and the existing speaking practice. There is no embedded video, creator/course link, external video request, registration or sales pitch in these English lessons. These are guided interactive lessons, not newly recorded human videos.

Four situations are available for both U.S. and U.K. English learning:

| Situation | Reply | Spanish meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meet someone | Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto. |
| Ask for repetition | Could you say that again, please? | ¿Podrías repetir eso, por favor? |
| Order a coffee | I'd like a coffee, please. | Quisiera un café, por favor. |
| Explain you are hungry | I'm hungry. | Tengo hambre. |

The Spanish explanation button speaks Spanish-only narration using es-EC, so English words are not passed through the Spanish voice. The example, reply and every chunk use the selected en-US/en-GB locale. These ordinary phrases work in both varieties; no claim is made that a phrase is exclusive to a country. Device voice availability/quality remains device-dependent. Speech is synthesized by the existing device mechanism, not a recording of an instructor or cloned speaker.

Three steps—Understand, Listen, Speak—keep the lesson focused. Learners can replay and move between steps without a timer. Speaking keeps the complete target visible. Feedback is the existing recognized-word comparison with an explicit qualification that it is not an accent/phoneme score. Done practising acknowledges the learner's choice to finish and does not claim mastery. It stores no new audio or proficiency result. Topic choices remain separate per English variety for the page session; changing language resets the mini lesson to its first step.

Step/topic/goal changes cancel old capture and model playback; closing the section or leaving Practice stops model audio. Active intermediate mini lessons defer automatic reload while the section is open on the active Practice view. Finishing or leaving Practice releases this additional protection. The saved learning goal and translator independence remain unchanged. Spanish learning retains its existing university recordings, captions and regional choice.
