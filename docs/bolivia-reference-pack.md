# Bolivian Spanish reference pack (v1)

Habla Ecuador keeps Ecuadorian Spanish first while making documented Bolivian usage searchable and visible beside translations. Native speakers may flag entries, but a speaker claim does not automatically change translations or constitute country-wide evidence.

## Sources and permissible use

* **ASALE — Diccionario de americanismos**: https://www.asale.org/damer/ — free to consult, copyrighted and all rights reserved. Use short original editorial paraphrases, country or regional sense citations, and links; do **not** bulk copy, scrape or redistribute definitions.
* **Wikcionario (Spanish Wiktionary)**: https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/Categor%C3%ADa:ES:Bolivia — CC BY-SA 4.0 content (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Selected *entry pages* can support dictionary senses; cite and link the specific pages. If copying or adapting actual text, provide appropriate attribution and comply with ShareAlike. This pack includes editorial wording and newly written examples, not copied entries. Editorial meanings are subject to local review.
* **RLA-ES, Hunspell Spanish Bolivia (es_BO)**: https://github.com/sbosio/rla-es — open-source spellchecking/word-form resources, not a translation model, dialect classifier, audio source or pronunciation model. Fedora identifies LGPL-3.0-or-later OR GPL-3.0-or-later OR MPL-1.1 packaging. The upstream's per-file requirements must be reviewed before embedding any .dic/.aff files. None are included in this release.
* **Los Tiempos (2023 regional reporting)**: https://www.lostiempos.com/node/608243/devel — contemporary reporting including the phrase *ch’aki* in a La Paz example; copyright retained by its publisher. Use source links and original editorial notes, not copied article content.

## What ships

1. A provenance-aware, searchable **casero/casera** pair: familiar market customer and familiar market seller, shared across Bolivia and other Andean countries, with the senses separate.
2. A **ch’aki** hangover entry with La Paz reporting and explicit limits on broader region claims.
3. In Translate, when source Spanish is set to **Bolivia**, evidence-linked notes about *casero/caserita*, *ch’aki*, and *yapa* can be shown for review. No automatic word replacement is applied.
4. Sources & coverage lists ASALE, Wikcionario, RLA-ES and Los Tiempos with import status and limits.

## What does NOT ship

No full dictionary mirror; no downloadable es_BO Hunspell spelling engine; no claim that these sources alone improve machine translation weights, audio or ASR; no native-speaker verification; no country-exclusive marker for a shared Andean word. These require separate legal and technical validation.

## Editorial evidence checks

Compare the actual local sense, not just the lemma. Record country and smaller region *only* if documented. Check spelling variants separately from meaning. Preserve the user's original input, draft and corrections. Keep native-speaker suggestions pending until research corroboration.
