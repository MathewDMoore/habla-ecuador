# Source and provenance policy

Habla Ecuador is Ecuador-first, but it must distinguish evidence from assumption.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Academia Ecuatoriana de la Lengua (AEL)** for Ecuador-focused lexicography.
2. **CORPHA** for real Ecuadorian usage, context, date, and regional evidence.
3. **ASALE Diccionario de americanismos** for cross-country sense and register comparisons.
4. **MITAD and other Ecuador-focused references** as discovery and corroboration sources when reuse terms permit.
5. **Academic research, Ecuadorian media, and historical references** for regional, cultural, and diachronic context.
6. **Native-speaker review** for naturalness, tone, and conversational appropriateness.

## Classification rule

Do not label a word **Ecuador-specific** solely because an Ecuadorian dictionary lists it.

Use:

- **Ecuador-specific** only when comparative evidence supports exclusivity or a highly localized sense.
- **Ecuador-associated** when strongly characteristic of Ecuador but also used elsewhere.
- **Regional Ecuador** when evidence identifies a subnational region.
- **Shared Andean** when Ecuador shares the usage with nearby Andean varieties.
- **General Spanish used in Ecuador** when it is normal Spanish but not Ecuador-distinctive.
- **Research lead** when confirmation is incomplete.

## Copyright and examples

Store concise paraphrases, metadata, and original learning examples. Do not reproduce substantial dictionary definitions, corpus passages, or proprietary lesson content.

## English policy

US and UK English should be translated independently for naturalness. They may be identical when there is no meaningful regional difference.

## Comparison policy

Bolivia is the primary comparison variety. Panama and other countries should be added when they reveal a useful contrast, false friend, register difference, or culturally meaningful usage.
