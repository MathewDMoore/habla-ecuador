How a song becomes language evidence

  1. Confirm the artist, Ecuador connection, genre, region, and date.
  2. Show full lyrics only when their license permits reuse; otherwise store a short observation and write an original example.
  3. Cross-check the meaning in CORPHA, the Ecuadorian Academy, ASALE, or another documented source.
  4. Label the register, intensity, generation, region, and whether it sounds natural in conversation.
  5. Write a new Habla Ecuador example and send uncertain usage for Ecuadorian review.
