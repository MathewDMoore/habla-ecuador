## Permission granted

The contributor gives Habla Ecuador a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to:

1. store the identified recordings;
2. play them inside the Habla Ecuador educational application and related demonstrations;
3. reproduce and distribute them only as embedded app assets, not as a standalone audio collection; and
4. make limited technical edits: trim silence, reduce background noise, convert file format, and normalize volume.

The recordings may be paired with written words, translations, pronunciation guidance, cultural notes, and learning exercises.

## Uses not permitted

This permission does **not** authorize:

- voice cloning or creation of a synthetic voice;
- training or fine-tuning a generative speech or artificial-intelligence model;
- impersonating the contributor;
- biometric identification or voiceprint analysis;
- advertising endorsements;
- changing the intended words or meaning;
- selling the recordings as standalone files; or
- using culturally restricted, sacred, private, or confidential material.
