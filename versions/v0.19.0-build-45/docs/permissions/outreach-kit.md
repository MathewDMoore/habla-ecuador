# Habla Ecuador — Rights and Collaboration Outreach Kit

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

Project contact: **Mathew Moore**  
Project: **Habla Ecuador** — an independent, Ecuadorian-first Spanish learning and translation prototype  
Repository: https://github.com/MathewDMoore/habla-ecuador

## Non-negotiable project safeguards

- Current project use is educational and noncommercial.
- No voice cloning, speaker impersonation, biometric identification, or unrelated AI training.
- No scraping, bulk copying, or republishing an existing course or dictionary.
- Only specifically identified files may be used.
- Audio edits are limited to silence trimming, gentle noise reduction, format conversion, and volume normalization unless separately approved.
- Speaker, rights-holder, and applicable community authorization must all be respected.
- Every external item must remain traceable, credited, and removable.
- A future commercial release would require a new written agreement unless the existing license expressly permits it.

## Response evaluation checklist

A positive response is not sufficient unless it identifies:

- the exact files or newly commissioned recordings;
- the person/entity authorized to license them;
- speaker consent;
- applicable community authorization;
- permitted app uses;
- whether format conversion and minor cleanup are allowed;
- attribution wording;
- commercial/noncommercial limits;
- duration/territory, if limited;
- removal procedure; and
- confirmation that the permission does not authorize voice cloning or model training.

Responses that say only “you may use our website,” “educational use is fine,” or “credit us” require follow-up clarification before any material is imported.
