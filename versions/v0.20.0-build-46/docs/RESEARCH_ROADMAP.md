# Priority research roadmap

Habla Ecuador's lexical work is priority one. The goal is to expand the app quickly without sacrificing evidence quality.

## Tier 1: Ecuador authority

1. Academia Ecuatoriana de la Lengua, Diccionario académico de ecuatorianismos.
2. CORPHA for authentic context, date, source type, and Ecuador region.
3. AEL's diatopic correction files to catch geographic-label gaps in broader dictionaries.

## Tier 2: Cross-country comparison

Use ASALE's Diccionario de americanismos to distinguish Ecuador-specific senses from shared Andean or wider Latin American usage. Bolivia is the primary comparison variety; Panama and others are added when a contrast is useful.

## Tier 3: Regional and cultural depth

- Carlos Joaquín Córdova, *El habla del Ecuador*, for historical and diachronic leads.
- *Hablemos Ecuatoriano* for contemporary colloquial discovery.
- Ecuadorian phraseology research, especially coastal and Manabí expressions.
- Research on Quichua/Kichwa influence in Ecuadorian Spanish.
- MITAD material supplied by the project owner as a discovery/corroboration source.

## Evidence workflow for each entry

1. Find a candidate in an Ecuador-focused source.
2. Check ASALE or another contrastive source before labeling it unique.
3. Query CORPHA and record region/date/source metadata.
4. Write original learner-facing examples.
5. Add natural US English and UK English independently.
6. Add Bolivia comparison if evidence supports it.
7. Add a regional Ecuador label only when the source supports one.
8. Mark native-speaker review separately from dictionary/corpus evidence.
